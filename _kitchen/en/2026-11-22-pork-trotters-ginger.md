Homemade pork trotters with ginger: the finished dish, sweet vinegar and preparation.

Known as *zhu jiao jiang* (猪脚姜), this Cantonese dish is traditionally prepared with pork trotters, ginger, eggs and sweet vinegar. In Lingnan food customs, ginger vinegar is also associated with celebrating a new baby and sharing food with relatives and friends.

The tradition has local heritage recognition: **the craft of making Nansha pork trotters with ginger** is listed in the seventh batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative projects in Nansha, Guangzhou. [Official listing](https://www.gzns.gov.cn/gznsqfb/gkmlpt/content/10/10908/post_10908831.html).
